How a deal earns its verdict

  • TRUE DEAL — the deepest cut on the board, a record-low price, or half off a real usual price.
  • STRONG PRICE — a solid cut worth buying, just not the lowest the item has ever sold for.
  • ROUTINE SALE — a small cut on a real price. Fine if you need it now, not a reason to rush.
  • WEAK — CHECK FIRST — the discount looks big against the list price, but the price is still high against its own history.
